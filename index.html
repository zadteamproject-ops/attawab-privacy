Attawab — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Attawab Android application, package name com.zadproject.attawab.

Summary

Attawab is an educational reading app. The app does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not ask users to provide personal information.

Information We Collect

The app does not ask users to provide personal information.

The app does not ask you to provide, or request device permissions to access:

- Name

- Email address, unless you choose to contact us by email

- Phone number

- Precise location

- Contacts

- Photos or media

- Microphone or camera data

- Payment information

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand basic app usage and stability, such as app opens, screen/activity usage, device/app information, and diagnostic events. This helps us improve the app experience and identify issues.

Firebase may process app-instance and device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available and permitted by device settings, as well as approximate location derived from network information. The app does not request GPS location access.

We do not use analytics for advertising, ad targeting, or selling user data.

Local App Data

Attawab stores app content locally on your device using a bundled SQLite/Room database. The app may also store a local date value to remember when content was last refreshed.

This local data stays on your device and is used only for app functionality.

Network Connections

The app uses internet access for these purposes:

- Downloading refreshed educational content from the app content API

- Loading author images from the public image host

The app can work from its bundled/local content. It may automatically check for refreshed educational content, and you may also manually request a content update from inside the app.

When the app connects to these services, normal technical information may be visible to the server or hosting provider, such as IP address, request time, URL requested, and basic device/network request metadata. We do not use this information to identify users.

Current external services used by the app include:

- Attawab content API: http://attawab.brainkets.com/apps/web/api/

- Author image hosting: https://zadteamproject-ops.github.io/attawab/authors-images/

- Google Analytics for Firebase

Firebase and Push Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging is used only if you enable notifications in the app. If enabled, the app may subscribe to notification topics through Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send app content or important update notifications.

The app does not upload Firebase registration tokens to the Attawab backend.

Email and Contact

The app may let you open your email app to contact us. If you choose to send an email, your email address and message are handled by your chosen email provider and are sent only because you initiated that action.

We use emails you send only to read and respond to your request.

Contact email:

info@attawab.brainkets.com

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share personal information with advertisers.

Technical request information may be processed by hosting providers only as necessary to provide the content API, image hosting, security, and server operation. Google may process analytics and Firebase Cloud Messaging data as part of providing Firebase services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because the app does not create user accounts and does not intentionally collect personal information, there is no app account data to delete.

You can delete local app data from your device at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage in Android settings.

If you contact us by email and want your email message deleted, you can request deletion by emailing us at:

info@attawab.brainkets.com

Security

The app uses network connections for app content, author images, Firebase services, and user-initiated email contact. Author images are loaded over HTTPS. The current Attawab content API uses HTTP at attawab.brainkets.com; we plan to use HTTPS when a valid certificate is available for that host.

We take reasonable steps to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

Children

Attawab is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes, including if new features, services, analytics, ads, accounts, Firebase, or push notifications are added.

When we update this policy, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact:

info@attawab.brainkets.com
